A handmade crochet bunny bag you open through the mouth. The rainbow-striped wool body holds your phone, cards, and daily essentials on a 116 cm adjustable strap.

How to Use/Style:
- Wear it crossbody over a hoodie or casual outfit for an instant kidcore or cottagecore look.
- Clip it to a tote handle for a bag-on-bag festival setup.
- Style it as a statement piece at anime conventions or themed events alongside other kawaii accessories.

Product Details
- Design: Full amigurumi bunny with tall pink ears, crocheted yellow hair, green flower accent, and red safety eyes. A tiny crocheted carrot sits in one paw.
- Opening: The wide pink mouth at the top of the bag is the entry point. Easy to access, easy to close with a quick tuck.
- Body: Horizontal rainbow stripes in soft multicolor wool running the full length.
- Strap: 116 cm (45.7 in) adjustable pink cord for crossbody or shoulder carry.
- Capacity: Fits most smartphones, a flat cardholder, folded cash, lip balm, keys, and earbuds. This is a compact essentials bag, not a full-size purse.
- Weight: 0.1 kg (3.5 oz).

Dimensions Top Width 11 cm, Bottom Width 12 cm, Height 29 cm, Depth 1 cm, Strap 116 cm.

Measurements may vary 1 to 3 cm. Each bag is crocheted by hand, so no two bunnies are exactly alike.

Care Instructions
Hand wash or gentle machine wash in cool water, below 40°C (104°F).
Wash separately or with similar light colors to prevent stripe bleeding.
Lay flat or hang to air dry.
Do not tumble dry as heat shrinks wool yarn.
Reshape gently while still damp.
