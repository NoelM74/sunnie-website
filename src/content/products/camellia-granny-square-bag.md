A crochet purse with a full 3D camellia bloom on the front, built to actually carry your phone and keys, not just sit on a shelf.

Hui works each bag in granny square stitch with an open shell-stitch panel through the lower half, then builds the camellia in spiraling layers and hand-attaches it to a leaf base. A matching flower bud sits on the green drawstring cord that cinches the scalloped top closed.

- **3D camellia flower:** hand-layered petals give the bag a real floral centerpiece, not a flat appliqué.
- **Drawstring closure:** a green cord threads through the scalloped rim and cinches shut, so nothing falls out.
- **Braided cream strap:** adjustable for crossbody or shoulder carry.
- **Holds a phone, cardholder, lip balm and keys:** enough for a night out without the big bag.
- **Four flower colours:** red, sage green, pink or orange, all on a cream base.

A pretty, practical pick for anyone who wants florals without the fuss, and an easy gift for a friend who likes cottagecore style.

*W 16 × H 15 cm. Each bag is crocheted by hand, so allow 1 to 3 cm either way.*
