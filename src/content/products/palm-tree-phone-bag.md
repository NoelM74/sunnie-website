This hand crocheted phone bag has a bright yellow striped body with a palm tree, sun and wave design on the front, made for anyone who loves a coastal look.

Each one is crocheted by hand in soft polyester yarn, with cream and yellow stripes running across the body and the beach scene worked in contrast yarn rather than printed on.

It closes with a small wooden button loop at the top and clips onto the strap with metal swivel hooks, so you can take it off the strap and carry it as a little pouch on its own.

There's enough room inside for a phone, lip balm and a lipstick, so it doubles as a coin purse and phone holder in one. It weighs just 110g, so you won't notice it on your shoulder even after a full day out.

Size:
- Top and bottom width: 13cm
- Height: 18cm
- Thickness: 1cm
- Strap length: 115cm

It's an easy grab for everyday errands when you only need your phone, cards and a lip balm on you, and it earns its keep on holidays and beach days when you want to travel light.

It also works nicely as a birthday, anniversary or just-because gift for anyone who likes handmade things.

Handmade notes:
Every bag is crocheted by hand, so small differences in stitch tension or motif placement are normal and part of what makes it yours.

- Please allow 1 to 3cm difference from the measurements above.

Care:
Hand wash in cool or lukewarm water, below 40C. Lay flat to dry and keep it out of strong sun for long periods so the colours stay true.
