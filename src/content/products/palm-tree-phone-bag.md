A bright little bag for your phone, lip balm and lipstick, small enough to vanish into a beach tote and cheerful enough to spot from across the sand.

Hui crochets the palm tree, sun and wave scene straight into the stitches in contrast yarn, so there's nothing printed on to crack or fade. A wooden button loop closes the top, and metal swivel hooks let you unclip the strap and carry it as a stand-alone pouch.

- **Scene worked into the stitches:** the palm tree, sun and wave are crocheted in contrast yarn, not printed on.
- **Wooden button loop:** closes the top without a zip to snag.
- **Metal swivel hooks:** unclip the 115 cm strap and carry it as a pouch on its own.
- **Room for the essentials:** a phone, lip balm and a lipstick fit inside.
- **About 110 g:** light enough that you forget it's there.

It earns its keep on beach days and holidays, when you want your hands free and nothing heavy on your shoulder. It also makes an easy birthday or just-because gift for anyone who likes a coastal look.

*W 13 cm, H 18 cm, D 1 cm, strap 115 cm. Measured by hand, so allow 1 to 3 cm either way.*
