A handmade crochet phone bag with a large 3D sculptural flower on the front. Available in yellow sunflower or pink rose on a cream base. Fits phones up to 6.5 inches.

How to Use/Style:
- Wear it crossbody over a white sundress or linen set for a farmers market or garden party look.
- Clip it against a plain black turtleneck for an everyday contrast that gets noticed.
- Pack it for festivals, picnics, or spring brunches. The drawstring top keeps everything in while your hands stay free.

Product Details:
- Design: Large 3D crochet flower hand-stitched onto a cream wool body with a green stem and leaves. The sunflower has layered yellow petals and a brown center. The rose has spiraling coiled pink petals worked flat and attached by hand.
- Closure: Drawstring cinch at the top. Pull to tighten, loosen to access.
- Strap: 110 cm (43.3 in) adjustable cord with lobster clasp for crossbody or shoulder carry.
- Capacity: Fits smartphones up to 6.5 inches, a flat cardholder, keys, lip balm, and earbuds.
- Weight: 0.08 kg (2.8 oz).

Dimensions: Width 12 cm, Height 18 cm, Depth 1 cm, Strap 110 cm.
 Measurements may vary 1 to 3 cm. Each bag is crocheted by hand.

Care Instructions:
- Hand wash or gentle machine wash in cool water, below 40°C (104°F).
- Wash separately from dark colors to prevent dye transfer.
- Lay flat or hang to air dry. Do not tumble dry.
- Spot clean with a damp cloth for minor marks.
