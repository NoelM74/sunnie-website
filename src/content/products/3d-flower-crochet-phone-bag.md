A crochet phone bag with a sculpted flower on the front, big enough to notice, small enough to wear every day.

Hui works the sunflower in layered rounds of yellow petals with a chenille brown centre, or coils the rose petal by petal in soft pink, then stitches it onto a cream base with a green stem and leaves. A drawstring pulls the top closed, and a lobster clasp keeps the strap secure.

- **Fits phones up to 6.5 inches:** plus a flat cardholder, keys, lip balm and earbuds.
- **110 cm adjustable strap:** lobster clasp for crossbody or shoulder wear.
- **Drawstring closure:** pull to close, loosen to reach in.
- **Two designs:** layered sunflower or coiled rose.
- **About 80 g:** light enough to forget you're wearing it.

A pretty everyday bag for anyone who likes flowers on their outfit, or a spring gift that arrives ready to wear.

*W 12 × H 18 × D 1 cm, strap 110 cm. Each flower is shaped by hand, so allow 1 to 3 cm of variation.*
