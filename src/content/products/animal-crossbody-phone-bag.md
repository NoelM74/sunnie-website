A crochet animal bag with a squishy white belly, big round eyes and legs that dangle when you walk, sized for the essentials and built to start conversations.

Hui crochets each character from soft polyester yarn, finished with a magnetic snap that keeps things closed without a zip to fight. Pick your favourite from four faces, each with its own personality.

- **Four characters:** pink cat, green frog, yellow duck or brown sloth.
- **Magnetic snap closure:** no zip, no fumbling, just snap shut.
- **110 cm adjustable strap:** sits crossbody on most teens and adults.
- **Holds the essentials:** phone, a few cards, keys and lip balm.
- **About 100 g:** light enough that you forget it's there.

A fun everyday bag for anyone who likes cute characters more than logos, and an easy gift for a friend who lights up over odd little animals.

*W 12 × H 19 × D 2 cm, strap 110 cm. Each bag is crocheted by hand, so faces, limb length and stitch tension vary slightly.*
