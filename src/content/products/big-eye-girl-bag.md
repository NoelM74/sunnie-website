A crochet bag with big cartoon eyes and fuzzy pigtail hair on top, made for anyone who wants a character bag instead of another plain purse.

Hui works the body in a flecked tweed-look yarn, so flashes of colour run through the fabric, then tops it with a separate curly hair piece styled into two pigtails with a small flower tucked in. The same drawstring cords that close the bag double as the crossbody strap.

- **Fits phone, lipstick, tissues, keys or a bus card:** plus room for coins and small toys.
- **110 cm strap:** the drawstring cords do double duty as the strap.
- **Four colourways:** purple, blue, pink or green, hair and flower matched to each.
- **About 140 g:** substantial without feeling heavy on the shoulder.
- **Tweed-flecked body with fuzzy pigtail hair:** a texture you won't find on a plain bag.

A fun first bag for kids or a quirky character piece for anyone who likes handmade oddities more than a logo.

*Bottom W 13 cm, top W 15 cm, H 25 cm, D 1 cm, strap 110 cm. Each bag is crocheted by hand, so allow 1 to 3 cm of variation, plus small differences in the hair texture.*
