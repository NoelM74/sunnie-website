This hand crocheted big eyed girl bag has fuzzy brown hair and twin pigtails on top, made for kids and adults who like a quirky character bag.

The body is worked in a flecked, tweed look yarn, so you get little flashes of colour running through the main fabric rather than one flat shade. On top sits a separate curly brown "hair" section, styled into two little pigtails with a small flower tucked in.

It closes with a drawstring rather than a button or zip, and those same cords double up as the crossbody strap. Two tiny crochet feet sit at the base, and everything, the feet, the flower and the drawstring cord, comes in a matching colour depending on which version you pick.

It comes in four colourways: purple, blue, pink and green.

Size:
- Top width: 15cm
- Bottom width: 13cm
- Height: 25cm
- Thickness: 1cm
- Strap length: 110cm

At 140g, it's light enough for kids to carry coins, small toys or sweets. It works just as well for teens or adults who want somewhere for a phone, lipstick, tissues, keys or a bus card.

It also makes a good birthday or anniversary gift for anyone who likes handmade, slightly odd little characters.

Handmade notes:
Each bag is crocheted by hand, so please allow 1 to 3cm difference from the measurements above. Expect small variations in the fuzzy hair texture and fleck pattern between bags too.

Screen settings and lighting can shift the colour slightly from what you see in photos.

Care:
Hand wash in cool or lukewarm water, below 40C. Lay flat to dry and keep it out of strong sun for long periods so the colours don't fade.
