A striped crochet tote roomy enough for an iPad, finished with a detachable flower charm you can swap on or off.

Hui works the body in cream acrylic yarn with blue stripes through solid and openwork rows, then finishes the base flat enough to stand on its own once packed. A braided cord carries a crocheted navy flower with a white centre and a navy and cream smiley charm, tied onto the handle, with a faux-leather "Hand made" label stitched near the top.

- **Fits an iPad, phone, paperback, small umbrella and a wallet.**
- **Magnetic snap closure:** keeps everything from sliding out.
- **Detachable flower and smiley charm:** 40 cm braided cord, on or off in seconds.
- **30 cm strap drop:** sits on the shoulder or in the crook of your arm.
- **About 140 g total:** light for how much it holds.

An everyday tote for the commute, market runs or coffee trips, and a good gift for anyone who likes a bit of charm on their bag.

*W 25 × H 27 × D 6 cm, strap drop 30 cm. Each bag is crocheted by hand, so stitch tension and charm shape vary slightly.*
