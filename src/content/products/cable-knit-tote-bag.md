A chunky cable knit tote with real structure, roomy enough for a laptop and everything else your day needs.

Hui knits the raised Aran braid pattern straight through the body and into the straps in one continuous piece, so the cables run unbroken from handle to base. The open top has no zip to fight, and the 5 cm base holds its shape once you load it up.

- **Fits a 13 to 15 inch laptop in a sleeve,** plus a 750 ml water bottle, a book, a wallet, keys, phone and sunglasses.
- **Integrated cable straps:** knitted as part of the bag, not sewn on separately.
- **27 cm handle drop:** sits comfortably on one shoulder.
- **About 260 g:** substantial without feeling heavy empty.
- **Three colours:** cream, khaki or terracotta.

A useful daily bag for cold weather commutes, market runs or library days, and a solid gift for anyone who carries a lot and wants it to look good.

*Top W 34 cm, bottom W 37 cm, H 35 cm, D 5 cm, handle drop 27 cm. Each bag is hand knitted, so allow small variations in tension and texture.*
