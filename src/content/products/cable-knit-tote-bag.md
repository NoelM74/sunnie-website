A chunky cable knit tote in soft acrylic yarn with a raised Aran braid pattern, roomy enough for a laptop, a water bottle, and everything else your day throws at you.

The shape is relaxed but the bag has real structure. The cable pattern runs all the way through the integrated straps, not just across the body. It sits comfortably on one shoulder and holds its shape once packed.

Three colors to choose from: Cream, Khaki, and Terracotta. All the same pattern, just different moods.

The open top means you can get in and out of it easily. No zipper to fumble with. If you need things secured, tuck a pouch inside.

Product details

- Material: soft acrylic yarn, chunky cable knit construction
- Pattern: raised Aran braid, continuous across body and straps
- Top width: 34cm, bottom width: 37cm, height: 35cm, depth: 5cm
- Handle drop: 27cm
- Weight: 0.26kg
- Open top, no closure
- Available in Cream, Khaki, and Terracotta
- Straps are integrated into the construction, not attached separately

What fits inside:
A 13 to 15 inch laptop in a sleeve fits comfortably. A 750ml water bottle, a book, a wallet, keys, a phone, sunglasses. The 5cm base depth gives the bag just enough structure to stop everything from going flat. Knit fabric has natural flex, so it conforms a little to whatever you put in it.

Handmade notes
- Each bag is hand-knited. Small variations in tension and texture are normal and part of what makes it handmade.
- Because this is a knit construction rather than woven, the bag has natural give and will flex with the weight of its contents.
- Colors may read slightly differently on different screens.

Care
- Hand wash in cool water below 40°C, or machine wash on a gentle cycle.
- Wash separately or with similar colors to avoid transfer.
- Keep away from items with zippers or rough textures that could snag the knit.
- Lay flat on a towel to air dry. Reshape while damp if needed.
- No tumble drying. No heat. Both will shrink the wool and distort the cable pattern.
