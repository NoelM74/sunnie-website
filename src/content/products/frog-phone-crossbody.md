Handmade construction
Each frog phone bag is crocheted by hand using amigurumi‑style techniques. The body is worked in continuous rounds and lightly stuffed for shape, then the facial features and outfit details are embroidered or stitched on and securely attached.

The detachable strap clips on with metal clasps so you can adjust the length or remove it entirely to use the frog as a clutch or pouch. No two frogs are exactly alike—small differences in eye placement, smile curve, and stitching give each one its own personality.

Care instructions
Hand wash gently in cool water with mild soap. Avoid harsh detergents or bleach. Gently squeeze out excess water without wringing or twisting, then lay flat on a clean towel to air dry, reshaping as needed.

Do not machine wash, tumble dry, or apply direct heat, as this can distort the shape and stress the seams.
