Four crochet flower coasters catch every ring your mugs would leave on the table, one in each of four bright colourways.

Hui shapes every coaster into five scalloped petals around a contrasting center, then finishes it with a 4 cm green stem and two little leaves. It measures 15 cm across and about 0.5 cm thick, with just enough curve to sit flat and steady under a mug or a candle.

- **Set of four colourways:** red with a cream edge, blue with teal, pink with sage green and yellow with a red edge, one of each.
- **Scalloped petal edge:** catches drips before they reach the wood.
- **About 20 g:** light, but substantial enough to stay put.
- **Stem and leaf detail:** a small decorative touch even when there's no mug on it.

A small, thoughtful gift for anyone who loves handmade kitchen things, and an easy stocking stuffer or housewarming add-on.

*Ø 15 cm each. Every coaster is crocheted by hand, so allow 1 to 2 cm either way.*
