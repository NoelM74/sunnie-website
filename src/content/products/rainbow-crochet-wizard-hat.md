A rainbow hat with a pointed peak and a star on top, easy to spot from across a busy playground and warm enough for actual winter wear.

Hui works the gradient stripes vertically from brim to peak in thick, soft acrylic yarn, then finishes the top with an 8 cm hand-stitched star. The ruffled brim stretches down over little ears to block the wind, and doubles as a costume piece for dress-up days.

- **Vertical rainbow stripes:** bold enough to spot your child on a crowded playground.
- **Stretchy ruffled brim:** pulls down over the ears to block cold wind.
- **8 cm star appliqué:** hand-stitched on at the peak.
- **Two sizes:** 2 to 5 years (48 cm brim) or 5 to 9 years (58 cm brim).
- **About 100 g:** light enough for all-day wear, indoors or out.

A photogenic winter gift for parents and grandparents to give, and a fun dress-up piece long after the cold snap passes.

*2 to 5 years: 48 cm brim. 5 to 9 years: 58 cm brim. Each hat is crocheted by hand, so stripe width and peak angle vary slightly.*
