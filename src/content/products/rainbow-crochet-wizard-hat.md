How to Style & Use It:
- Daily Wear: Perfect for chilly school mornings, winter park outings, or keeping heads warm during snowy adventures.
- Weather Protection: The stretchy ruffled brim pulls down easily over the ears to actively block out cold wind.
- Visibility: The bold, vertical gradient stripes make it incredibly easy to spot your child on a crowded playground or busy sidewalk.

About Our Process:
Each piece is carefully crocheted by hand from soft acrylic yarn. We meticulously work the vertical gradient stripes from the brim to the peak to match traditional fantasy shapes. Because we make them by hand, slight variations in stripe width and peak angle occur, making every single hat unique.

Care Instructions:
- Hand wash or machine wash on a cool cycle (below 40°C / 104°F).
- Wash separately from dark colors and rough items (like zippers) to prevent yarn snagging.
- Reshape gently while damp and lay flat to dry. Do not bleach.
