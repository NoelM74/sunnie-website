This handmade crochet poodle bag is fluffy from every angle and soft to hold, and it looks just as cute in white, honey yellow, or caramel.

Each poodle bag wears three tiny hair clips on top, and comes with its own little comb so you can fluff up the fur again once it arrives.

Inside, there's room for a phone, lipstick, tissues, keys, or a bus card. Kids often use it for coins, small toys, or candy, so it works well as a first "big kid" bag too, not just a display piece for adults.
