A fluffy crochet poodle that carries your phone, keys and lipstick, and arrives with its own little comb.

Hui crochets each poodle from soft plush yarn, then finishes it with three tiny hair clips on top. When a busy week flattens the fur, a few strokes with the comb bring it back.

- **Holds the everyday essentials:** phone, lipstick, tissues, keys and a bus card, so the big bag can stay at home.
- **110 cm adjustable strap:** wear it crossbody with both hands free, or shorten it for the shoulder.
- **Button closure:** everything stays put when you're on the move.
- **About 120 g:** you'll barely notice it's on.
- **Three colours:** honey yellow, cream white or caramel.

For kids it makes a proud first "big kid" bag for coins, small toys and sweets. For grown-ups it's a soft, silly bit of joy on a grey day.

*W 17 cm at the base, 14 cm at the top, H 15 cm, D 1 cm. Measured by hand, so allow 1 to 3 cm either way.*
