A crochet flower bag you can carry two ways: by its own small crocheted handle, or crossbody on the long clip-on strap.

Hui crochets each bag by hand in polyester yarn, with a vest-shaped top and rows of flowers. A wooden button closes the top, so your things stay secure without a zipper. The strap is fully detachable, so the bag changes from a top-handle clutch to a crossbody bag when you need it.

- **Three colours:** green with orange flowers, blue with red flowers, or purple with pink flowers, all the same price.
- **Two ways to carry:** crocheted top handle, or the 120 cm detachable strap.
- **Wooden button closure:** no zipper.
- **Holds the basics:** phone, lipstick, tissues, keys or a bus card.
- **Light to carry:** about 90 g.

A good pick if you like crochet or floral accessories. It also works as a birthday, anniversary or holiday gift, and as a first "big kid" bag for coins, small toys and snacks.

*W 16 cm, H 19 cm, D 1 cm, strap 120 cm. Measured by hand, so allow 1 to 3 cm either way. Colours can look slightly different on screen.*
