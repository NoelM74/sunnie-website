A soft, fluffy crochet bag that looks like a small plush friend: a white puppy, a yellow bear or a brown cat.

Hui crochets each one by hand in polyester plush yarn, so it is soft from every angle. The puppy has long black floppy ears, a small blue hat and a tiny crocheted strawberry cake. The bear is buttery yellow, with a blue-and-white striped hat and a little plate of chocolate cookies. The cat is brown plush with a brown-and-white polka dot bow.

- **Three designs:** white puppy, yellow bear or brown cat, all the same price.
- **Holds the basics:** phone, lipstick, tissues, keys or a bus card.
- **Adjustable strap:** 120 cm of brown PU leather, for shoulder, hand or crossbody wear.
- **Light to carry:** about 150 g.
- **Good for kids too:** they use it for coins, small toys or sweets, so it works as a first "big kid" bag.

A gift for anyone who likes fluffy character accessories, for a birthday, an anniversary or a holiday. Teens get a bag nobody else has.

*Top W 17 cm, bottom W 19 cm, H 16 cm, D 3 cm, strap 120 cm. Measured by hand, so allow 1 to 3 cm either way. Colours can look slightly different on screen.*
