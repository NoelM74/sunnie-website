A handmade crochet crossbody phone bag with an open knit pattern, tassel and wood bead detail, made for everyday carry and as a gift for anyone who loves boho style.

The outer knit layer has a canvas inner lining so small items stay put, and a magnetic snap closure keeps your phone and cards from slipping out through the open stitch gaps. It comes with two removable straps, a woven knit strap and a faux leather strap, so you can switch the look.

Details
- Material: hollow crochet knit outer, canvas lining, faux leather strap
- Colour: cream white or khaki
- Size: W 14.5 × H 20 × D 1.5 cm, strap 120 cm
- Weight: about 0.2 kg
- Closure: magnetic snap
- Extras: wood bead and tassel, small Hand Made tag

Light enough to wear crossbody or over the shoulder for festivals, beach days, holidays or a quick errand run.

Each bag is hand knitted, so the stitch pattern and tassel vary a little from piece to piece. Measurements are taken by hand, so allow 1 to 3 cm of difference, and colours may look slightly different depending on your screen.

- Lay flat to dry
- Keep out of long direct sunlight to protect the colour
