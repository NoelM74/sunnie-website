Hand-crocheted green bucket backpack with a ring of yellow sunflowers around the top and a drawstring closure with a crocheted leaf pull. Wears as a backpack or slung over one shoulder. Fits books, a tablet, phone, and an umbrella.

Product details
- Material: wool yarn, crocheted throughout. No lining visible in photos. Single open compartment inside.
- Dark green body, dense stitch pattern, round flat base. A circle of crocheted sunflowers with brown centers and yellow petals rings the top opening, connected by green leaf vines. The flowers sit upright and give the bag the look of a little garden pot.
- Drawstring closure with a green crocheted cord. The cord ends in a small crocheted leaf charm that hangs at the front.
- Two green crocheted shoulder straps attach at the top with small yellow metal D-rings. Wear both straps as a backpack or pull them together for a single-shoulder carry.
- Width 20 cm, height 23 cm, depth 20 cm. This is a roomy round bag. It holds books, a notebook, an iPad, a phone, an umbrella, and other daily things without much trouble.
- Weight about 340 g.

Great for
Daily commute, weekend outings, or a casual vacation bag. It holds more than you'd expect from the outside, and the sunflowers get noticed without being loud about it.

A thoughtful birthday or holiday gift for someone who likes sunflowers or handmade accessories. It's the kind of bag people ask about.

Handmade notes
Each bag is crocheted by hand, so sunflower count, petal shape, strap length, and overall dimensions will vary a little from piece to piece.

* Allow 1 to 3 cm difference. Colors on your screen may not match the yarn exactly.

Care
- Hand wash in cool or warm water below 40 degrees C.
- Reshape while damp and lay flat to dry.
- Avoid prolonged direct sunlight to prevent the green and yellow from fading.
