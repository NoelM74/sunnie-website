Handmade crochet coasters shaped like a pig, bear or cat, made as a birthday, anniversary, housewarming or holiday gift for friends and family, each with a fruit belly design and sold individually or as a set of three.

Choose the pink pig with a watermelon centre, the brown bear with an orange slice, or the beige cat with a kiwi, or get all three together. The soft yarn body cushions glasses and mugs and protects the table underneath, with four round legs, ears and a tail giving each coaster its 3D shape.

Details
- Material: soft yarn
- Design: pig with watermelon, bear with orange, or cat with kiwi
- Size: about 15 cm across and 3.5 cm thick, around 60 g each
- Features: safety eyes, embroidered nose, mouth and whiskers
- Set: sold individually or as a set of three

Good on coffee mugs, water glasses, tea cups and candles, and a welcome birthday, anniversary, housewarming or holiday gift.

Each coaster is hand crocheted, so size can vary by 1 to 3 cm, and colours may look slightly different depending on lighting and screen settings.

Care
Hand wash in cool or lukewarm water below 40 C, lay flat to dry, keep out of direct sunlight.
