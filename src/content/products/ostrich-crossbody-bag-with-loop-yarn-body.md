A shaggy loop-yarn ostrich wearing a tiny crown, roomy enough for your phone and just goofy enough that everyone asks about it.

Hui crochets the body in a loop stitch for a fluffy, scruffy texture, tops it with a white head in a yellow crown, and lets the yellow legs dangle from the bottom.

- **Open top, reach right in:** no zip, snap or drawstring.
- **Fits a phone, keys, lip balm, a small tin and cards:** not a full iPad or magazine.
- **108 cm fixed strap:** crossbody length for kids or adults.
- **About 140 g:** light for a bag with this much texture.
- **Three colours:** brown, pink or black, each with the same white head and yellow legs.

Works at markets, on walks or running around, for anyone who wants a bag with personality. A good birthday gift for bird lovers or anyone who wants a bag that's a little goofy.

*Top opening 15 cm, bottom W 14 cm, H 11 cm, D 14 cm, strap 108 cm. Hand crocheted, so allow 2 to 3 cm either way.*
