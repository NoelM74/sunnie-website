A crochet bag built entirely from tiny flowers, small enough for a phone and a lip balm, bright enough to be the outfit.

Hui crochets each flower separately in pink, red, orange, yellow, green, blue, sage and lilac, then joins them into a bag with a pearl bead at the opening. The 120 cm strap clips on and off, in a pink or teal colourway, so you can swap it to match your outfit.

- **Built from hand-crocheted flowers:** eight colours worked into one bag, no two exactly alike.
- **Detachable 120 cm strap:** pink or teal, unclips at the hook for easy swapping.
- **Holds a phone, lip balm, keys, coins and a small perfume:** kids use it for sweets and tiny toys too.
- **About 0.07 kg:** the lightest bag in the lineup.
- **12 × 18 × 1 cm:** small enough to layer over any outfit.

A bright pick for beach days, festivals and markets, and an easy birthday or anniversary gift for anyone who likes colour.
