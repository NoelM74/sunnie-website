A striped crochet tote with a detachable flower and heart charm, roomy enough for a tablet and daily essentials.

Hui works the body in cream yarn with navy stripes through an open-weave stitch, and crochets the base 6 cm thick so the bag stands upright on its own. A braided cord carries a chunky blue flower with a cream centre and a stuffed cream heart, and the charm lifts off if you'd rather wear the bag plain. A small tan leather-look "Hand made" label sits near the top edge.

- **Fits an iPad, a paperback, a phone, an umbrella and keys.**
- **Detachable flower and heart charm:** 40 cm cord, on or off.
- **30 cm handle drop:** comfortable for a full day out.
- **About 140 g total,** bag plus charm.

A day bag for commutes, markets or a weekend away, and a thoughtful gift for anyone who likes a small charm as much as the bag.

*W 25 × H 27 × D 6 cm, handle drop 30 cm. Each bag is crocheted by hand, so proportions vary slightly.*
