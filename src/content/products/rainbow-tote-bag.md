A hand-crocheted open-mesh tote in rainbow colorblock stripes, roomy enough for real life, with a little heart coin purse that clips on and comes off whenever you want.

Pick your palette. The warm colorway runs through blush, yellow, pink, and blue. The cool one goes white, yellow, purple, lavender, and teal.

Both have the same open mesh body, the same soft double straps finished in yellow, and the same generous interior that swallows a book, a tablet, an umbrella, a wallet, and a phone without complaint.

The heart shaped coin purse is a lovely bonus. It has a ruffled white edge, clips on with a metal ring, and holds the small stuff: cards, lip balm, coins, earbuds.

Move it, remove it, do whatever you like with it. A puff flower charm also comes along for the ride, color a surprise.

Product details
- Two colorways: warm rainbow (blush, yellow, pink, blue) or cool rainbow (white, yellow, purple, lavender, teal)
- Bag dimensions: approx. 31cm wide x 27cm tall x 6cm deep base
- Shoulder strap length: approx. 48cm
- Open mesh body with solid-crocheted top edge and yellow-trimmed double straps
- Removable heart coin purse: approx. 8x11cm, ruffled white edge, metal clip ring
- One puff flower charm included, color assigned at random
- Material: yarn (see Handmade notes for fiber detail)
- Weight: approx. 0.2kg
What fits inside A hardback book, an iPad, a folded umbrella, a phone, and a wallet all fit comfortably. The opening is wide and the base has real depth, so it does not look stuffed even when it is full.
