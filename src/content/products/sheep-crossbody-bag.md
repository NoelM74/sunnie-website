This crochet sheep crossbody bag is a 3D lamb character phone purse with a fluffy textured head and compact main compartment. Cream and dark brown wool‑blend yarn create a cute sheep with bobble‑stitch “wool,” dangling legs, and a dark brown rope strap for shoulder or crossbody wear.

Sized for a phone and a few essentials, it’s a fun everyday mini bag for animal‑loving adults and teens.

- Width: approx. 18 cm (7.1 in)
- Height: approx. 11 cm (4.3 in)
- Depth: approx. 4 cm (1.6 in)

 Manual measurements may vary by 1–3 cm due to the handmade construction.

Design & construction:
The head of the sheep is crocheted with a bobble or popcorn stitch to create a fluffy, curly wool effect, while the body is worked in a smoother stitch to form the main compartment. Four crocheted legs dangle from the bottom of the body, with two small ears on the sides of the head and a tail at the back.

A dark brown crocheted rope strap is attached to the top corners of the bag for crossbody or shoulder carry. A small button secures the opening at the top of the body.

Because each bag is handmade, slight variations in stitch tension, leg placement, ear size, “wool” fluffiness, and button position are normal and make every sheep unique.

What fits inside:
This is a compact phone bag designed for essentials only. It fits most smartphones up to around 6.7 inches (screen size), plus a few small flat items such as keys, a slim cardholder or folded cash, lip balm, and earbuds.

The body of the sheep (about 18 × 11 cm) opens at the top with the button closure. Due to the 3D shape and limited height, it’s best for a phone plus a small selection of lightweight essentials rather than a full wallet or bulky items.

This sheep bag is ideal for animal lovers, sheep and lamb enthusiasts, cottagecore and farmcore outfits, kawaii accessories, and anyone who loves quirky handmade character bags.

It makes a fun compact phone bag for casual daily carry, festivals, markets, and days out, as well as a cute gift for teens, birthdays, or anyone who appreciates handmade 3D crochet bags.

Care instructions:
Hand wash gently in cool water (below 30°C / 86°F) with mild soap if needed. Gently squeeze out excess water without wringing or twisting. Lay flat on a towel to air dry and reshape, especially around the fluffy bobble‑stitch head, which can be gently fluffed while damp.

Do not machine wash, tumble dry, or apply direct heat, and avoid washing with items that have zippers or hooks that could snag the crochet stitches or pull on the legs.
