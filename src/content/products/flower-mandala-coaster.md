This flower mandala coaster stops your mug leaving a ring, and looks good enough to leave out when there's no mug on it.

Hui hooks each coaster in a sunburst pattern, with a white petal ring, tiny green leaves and a scalloped edge in your chosen colour. The stitch sits soft and slightly squishy, so it grips the table and holds a heavy mug steady, while soaking up condensation before it reaches the wood.

- **Eight colourways:** yellow, purple, pink, red, or an ombre version in yellow, purple, pink or blue.
- **Grips the table:** soft, slightly squishy stitch keeps mugs and glasses from sliding.
- **Doubles as a doily:** use it under a candle, a small plant pot, or your rings and earrings at night.
- **Packs of 4, 6 or 8:** every pack includes a free mini crochet flower basket, one with the 4 or 6 pack, two with the 8 pack.

A colourful pick for a kitchen table, nightstand or work-from-home desk, and a thoughtful small gift for anyone you don't want to visit empty-handed.

*Each coaster is hooked by hand, one at a time, so small size differences are part of the look.*
