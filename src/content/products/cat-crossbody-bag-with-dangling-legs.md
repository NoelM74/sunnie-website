A crochet cat with a big wide mouth, a little sweater and long legs that dangle as you walk. The mouth is the opening of the bag.

Hui crochets each cat by hand in polyester yarn. Every cat has big round eyes, so they all look a bit cheeky. The arms and legs hang loose, and the strap matches the colour of the body.

- **Black cat:** pink sweater with a small blue fish, and an orange mouth.
- **Brown cat:** cream and tan striped sweater with a tiny yellow face on the chest, and an orange mouth.
- **Yellow cat:** blue sweater with a small pink fish, and a pink mouth.
- **Three ways to carry:** crossbody, on one shoulder or in the hand, on a 120 cm strap.
- **Holds the small things:** lipstick, tissues, keys or a bus card.
- **Light to carry:** about 170 g.

All three cats are the same price. Younger kids use the bag for coins, small toys, sweets and snacks. It also works as a gift for a birthday, an anniversary or a holiday.

*W 14 cm, H 30 cm including the dangling legs, D 1 cm, strap 120 cm. Measured by hand, so allow 1 to 3 cm either way. Colours can look slightly different on screen.*
