A cotton mushroom bag with its own drawstring top, roomy enough for a phone without losing its mushroom shape.

Hui crochets the cap in red with white spots and a tiny yellow-centred flower, closes it with braided cotton drawstring ties, then builds the stem below in cream cotton.

- **Drawstring closure:** pull the braided cotton ties shut in one motion.
- **Room for a phone, keys, cards and lip balm:** one soft compartment, no dividers.
- **120 cm strap:** worn crossbody or over the shoulder.
- **About 170 g:** substantial without feeling heavy.
- **Bounces back if squashed:** reshape it gently after transit and it returns to form.

A fun gift for anyone who collects mushroom things, or just wants a bag with a bit more personality than a plain crossbody.

*Top W 7 cm, bottom W 13 cm, H 16 cm, D 13 cm, strap 120 cm. Hand crocheted, so allow 1 to 3 cm either way.*
